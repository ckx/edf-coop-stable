# EDF6Coop 3.3.0

Press E while you are down to switch the spectator camera between free and locked.

- Free (the default) is the camera from 3.1.0: it orbits the watched teammate and points where you look, turned with your own look controls.
- Locked is the camera from before: the game's own view of the teammate, looking where they look. E switches back and forth; the choice lasts until you quit the game.
- E follows the W key's rules: keyboard only, not rebindable, ignored while the chat box is open, while another window has the focus and while you are standing.

Why the old camera sometimes pointed the wrong way: it shows the teammate's aim as the game receives it. On foot that arrives every sixth sync frame, so the view turns in steps of up to 100 ms and holds through a lost packet. While the teammate rides a vehicle or is down the game does not send their aim at all, and boarding resets it to straight ahead along the body's heading, so the locked view stays fixed while the vehicle moves. That comes from static analysis of `EDF.dll` and a read-only capture; a live check is still needed. While locked, `EDF6Coop.log` now notes when the watched teammate's aim has not changed for 120 frames, so a wrong direction can be told from a missing one. If you see one, note the time, whether the teammate was in a vehicle, and attach the log (its lines start with `SPECTATE`).

Netcode protocol 4 and the wire format are unchanged: 3.3.0 plays with 3.0.1 to 3.2.1.

This package is the clang/MinGW-w64 build tested under Wine, not an MSVC release build. All 29 non-stress tests passed under Wine, including the tests against the real `EDF.dll` and the multi-process rooms.

Extract the whole package and run `INSTALL.bat` on Windows or `bash install.sh` on Linux. Existing settings and other mods are preserved. Proton needs the Steam launch option `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winmm=n,b" %command%`.
