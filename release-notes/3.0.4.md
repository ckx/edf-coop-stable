# EDF6Coop 3.0.4

Fixes the player jitter that grew worse through 3.0.x, from the host's motion traces of 2026-10-22.

- A correction the physics does not apply (a landing, a stagger, the ground under a downward push) was read as the other player's copy moving the other way, and the next correction was made larger to fight it. Copies of players standing nearly still were shoved up and down 35 cm at 30 Hz, and landed copies were pushed at the 60 m/s cap. The estimator now counts a correction as applied only as far as the copy moved along it, over the last four frames, so a blocked correction stays at the size the position error asks for.
- The velocity a player sends was measured over the 33 ms since its previous write, while the others receive one position in three, every 100 ms. The start of a dash exceeded the speed cap within 33 ms and went out as "stopped", so the copy stood still for 100 ms and was then several metres off. The velocity now covers the 100 ms the receivers extrapolate over, and the cap is 120 m/s.
- On a steady direct link the retransmission timeout equalled the round trip plus 5 ms, and a host resent a quarter of its packets to a 180 ms joiner for nothing (the duplicates were dropped). The timeout now keeps a margin of a quarter of the round trip.

Netcode protocol 4 and the wire format are unchanged: 3.0.4 plays with 3.0.1 to 3.0.3, and a mixed room improves for the players who update (each machine's own copies and its own sent velocity). Update everyone for the full effect. The player motion diagnostics stay on by default and keep the same format.

What the logs did not show: the sessions traced had a lossless direct link with samples arriving within a few milliseconds of their fastest. If shots of other players still go missing or point the wrong way with everyone on 3.0.4, note the time and weapon, then close the game and collect every player's `Mods/Plugins/EDF6Coop.log`.

This package is the clang/MinGW-w64 build tested under Wine, not an MSVC release build. All 27 non-stress tests passed under Wine, including the 15 against the real `EDF.dll` and the eight multi-process rooms; the player sync tests gained the blocked correction, the push the ground undoes, the braked copy, the repeated step and the dash-start velocity. Live multiplayer improvement is unverified.

Extract the whole package and run `INSTALL.bat` on Windows or `bash install.sh` on Linux. Existing settings and other mods are preserved. Proton needs the Steam launch option `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winmm=n,b" %command%`.
