# EDF6Coop 3.8.0

Other players' facing turns every frame toward their last received aim, instead of jumping when a packet arrives. Until now a remote player's aim angles changed only when an aim record arrived, about every 100 ms. 3.8.0 writes the angles of every on-foot remote player each frame, moving toward the last received aim with a 50 ms time constant by the shorter way round. A change of more than 90 degrees, such as an about-turn, is shown at once. The spectator camera reads these angles, so a watched teammate turns the same way.

- Seated and downed players are not written: their game sends no aim then, and their angles stay with the game. So does a player whose aim has not arrived for 400 ms.
- Only your own screen changes, and nothing new is sent. It runs while the room has `[Netcode] PlayerSync` on. A sender on any build from 3.0.1 on is driven; one before 3.6.3 sends its aim only every sixth frame, so each aim is held longer.
- `[NetPlayer] DriveAim=1` turns it on; `0` leaves the angles to the game, as before. `AimTauMs=50` is the time constant in milliseconds (10 to 1000): smaller follows sooner, larger is smoother. An INI from before 3.8.0 has no `[NetPlayer]` section and uses these defaults; the section appears only in a newly written INI.
- For a capture: the `PLAYER sync` summary, every 30 seconds, counts the aim frames driven and the snaps. The aim diagnostic's `update` record shows the angles written that frame (`written=(pitch,yaw)`) and whether the frame was driven (`driven=1`).

Netcode protocol 4 and the wire format are unchanged: 3.8.0 plays with 3.0.1 to 3.7.0.

This package is the clang/MinGW-w64 build tested under Wine. The MSVC build and its non-stress tests were also run. Under Wine the 31 non-stress tests passed, including the 18 against the real `EDF.dll`. In the two-machine GameNet case each machine's player turns, its records go through the installed hooks, and the other machine's copy follows the aim smoothed, with the about-turn shown at once. Whether a teammate's facing and shot direction follow their aim in live play is not yet verified. Keep `[Diagnostics] Player=1` on every machine; if a teammate faces or shoots the wrong way, note the time, the player and the spectator camera mode, close the game and collect everyone's `EDF6Coop.log`.

Extract the whole package and run `INSTALL.bat` on Windows or `bash install.sh` on Linux. Existing settings and other mods are preserved. Proton needs the Steam launch option `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winmm=n,b" %command%`.
