# EDF6Coop 3.1.0

Adds the spectator camera, formerly the separate EDF6Spectate plugin, so one install does both.

- Online, while you are down, your view switches to the standing teammate with the lowest slot and moves to the next one when they go down or leave. The camera orbits them and points where you look; turn it with your normal look controls. Being revived, or every teammate being down, returns your own camera.
- The watched teammate gets a health bar, and the multiplayer name/health indicators stay. Ammo, radar and class gauges are not shown for them.
- Only your own screen changes. Nothing is sent to other players, and offline play and split screen are left alone. There is no button to pick a teammate yet.
- On by default. `[Display] SpectateWhenDown=0` in `EDF6Coop.ini` keeps your own camera.
- An installed `EDF6Spectate.dll` is renamed to `.disabled`, at the first start and by the installers, since both would take the same game call. Its leftover readme and notices are removed.
- The Chinese and Japanese readmes are gone; `README_EDF6Coop.txt` is the only one in the package. The settings file comments and installer messages stay in Chinese, Japanese and English.

Netcode protocol 4 and the wire format are unchanged: 3.1.0 plays with 3.0.1 to 3.0.4.

What is verified: following a teammate and returning to your own camera worked online with EDF6Spectate 0.1.1. The orbit camera and the health bar have not been checked online, in EDF6Spectate 0.3.0/0.4.0 or here. If the camera misbehaves, note the time and attach `Mods/Plugins/EDF6Coop.log` (the camera's lines start with `SPECTATE`).

This package is the clang/MinGW-w64 build tested under Wine, not an MSVC release build. All 29 non-stress tests passed under Wine, including the tests against the real `EDF.dll` and the multi-process rooms; the plugin load tests check that the camera's call is taken, left alone when it is off, and that an old `EDF6Spectate.dll` is retired.

Extract the whole package and run `INSTALL.bat` on Windows or `bash install.sh` on Linux. Existing settings and other mods are preserved. Proton needs the Steam launch option `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winmm=n,b" %command%`.
