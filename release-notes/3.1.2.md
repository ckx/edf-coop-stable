# EDF6Coop 3.1.2

The spectator camera shows the chat log and your own name indicator.

- While you watch a teammate, the chat log is visible. It shows the messages received since the spectator camera was made (your first death in the mission); your own camera keeps the full log and shows it again when you are revived.
- The name/health indicators now treat the watched teammate as their viewer. Your own downed body and the other teammates get indicators; the watched teammate, who fills the middle of the view, does not. 3.1.0 and 3.1.1 showed the watched teammate and hid your own body.
- Ten more ranges of the game's chat code are checked by hash before the camera is installed. If another mod changed them, the spectator camera stays off and `EDF6Coop.log` names the range; everything else runs.

Both changes come from static analysis of `EDF.dll` and have not been checked online. If the chat or an indicator does something wrong, note the time and attach `EDF6Coop.log` (its lines start with `SPECTATE`).

Netcode protocol 4 and the wire format are unchanged: 3.1.2 plays with 3.0.1 to 3.1.1.

This package is the clang/MinGW-w64 build tested under Wine, not an MSVC release build. All 29 non-stress tests passed under Wine, including the tests against the real `EDF.dll` and the multi-process rooms.

Extract the whole package and run `INSTALL.bat` on Windows or `bash install.sh` on Linux. Existing settings and other mods are preserved. Proton needs the Steam launch option `WINEDLLOVERRIDES="winmm=n,b" %command%`.
